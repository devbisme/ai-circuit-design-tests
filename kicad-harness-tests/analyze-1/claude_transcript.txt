  Design findings:
  - FT2232H channel B: it carries the FPGA flash programming. FTDI's AN_130 says
    it is unavailable while channel A is in sync-FIFO mode, so the host must
    leave that mode before reprogramming or resetting the FPGA. The FT2232H's
    configuration EEPROM also starts blank and must be set to FIFO mode first.
  - ±4 V supply (LM27762): your TODO is resolved; the dividers give +3.97 V and
    −4.03 V per the TI datasheet. Minor:
    - R406 and R408 (49.9 k) are just under the 50 k minimum.
    - PGOOD should be tied to GND.
    - C407 (4.7 µF, 0603) may lose too much capacitance at its operating
      voltage.
  - FPGA core decoupling: the 1.2 V caps sit 6–18 mm from the VCC pins, and 1.2
    V is routed as traces, not a plane.
  - Analog front end: the +3V3 plane covers the whole board, including under the
    inputs, and the high-impedance divider traces are 36–39 mm long. The impact
    isn't quantified.

    they are filled or tented.

    risk is low.
