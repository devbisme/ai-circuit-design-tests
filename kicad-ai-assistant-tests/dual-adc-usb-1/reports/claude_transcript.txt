● I've run every step of analysis_prompt.txt: the review, JLCPCB gerbers, BOM
  and pick-and-place files, the PCB PNG, and executive_summary.md. The design
  files weren't touched; their checksums match before and after.

  Review results. ERC and DRC (KiCad 10) show no errors and no unconnected
  items; the warnings are mostly silkscreen and library-setup noise. The issues
  worth acting on:
  - FPGA clock and ADC power at startup. The 10 MHz oscillator and the ADC both
    run on a rail that only switches on after USB enumeration. Until then the
    FPGA has no clock, and FPGA pins at 3.3 V can back-power the unpowered ADC.
  - ADC input overdrive. Above roughly ±38 V at the BNC, the amplifier can push
    the ADC inputs past their absolute maximum. I'm working from memory for that
    limit, because the LTC2290 datasheet wouldn't download.
  - Decoupling is far from the pins. Capacitors sit 10–22 mm from the FPGA, ADC,
    FT2232H and SDRAM supply pins. This is the board's biggest physical
    weakness.
  - Analog input routing. The ADC's anti-alias capacitors are 15–18 mm from its
    pins, and those traces run on the back layer next to the +3V3 plane rather
    than ground.
  - LM27762 (the ±4 V supply). Two small departures from its datasheet, both
    confirmed: the bottom feedback resistors are 49.9 kΩ where it requires at
    least 50 kΩ, and the unused PGOOD pin is left open instead of grounded.
  - Diode clamp is correct. KiCad's pin names make the BAV199DW clamp look
    miswired; the Diodes datasheet shows it is right.
  - Manufacturing:
    - Silkscreen lines are 0.12 mm, below JLC's ≈0.15 mm.
    - There are no part numbers or LCSC numbers, so the BOM can't be ordered
      as-is.
    - No stackup is defined.
    - Footprints aren't marked SMD or through-hole, so I left the 4 through-hole
      connectors out of the assembly files and listed them separately.

  How well kcaa did. kcaa's ERC, design-rule, zone and outline tools worked.
  Most of its analysis tools failed or gave wrong answers on this design:
  - Netlist extraction returned no pins at all.
  - Connection and pattern tools look at only one sheet at a time.
  - Ratsnest falsely reported 6 unconnected +4V connections; DRC shows 0.
  - BOM export failed because it calls the system KiCad 9 kicad-cli and ignores
    the configured KiCad 10 path.
  - Board info reports 0 copper layers on this 4-layer board.
  - DRC can't run without the KiCad window open.

  So most of the real review, and all the fab outputs, came from KiCad 10's
  kicad-cli, two small read-only scripts, and the datasheets. The summary marks
  which claims are confirmed against datasheets and which are from memory: the
