  Real issues

  1. Routing breaks the 0.2 mm rules. After refilling the zones, DRC reports 500
     clearance errors, all between 0.152 and 0.200 mm. There are also 14 track
     segments at 0.15 mm, on OSC_OUT, VCCPLL0/1 and OSCI. It looks like the
     router ran with ~0.15 mm rules. Two ways to fix it:
     - Lower the rules to 0.15 mm. This is quick and fine at most 4-layer fabs,
       though I haven't checked any specific fab's current rules.
     - Reroute at 0.2 mm. You keep the margin, but it costs a routing pass.
  2. The zone fill saved in the board is stale. It causes 237 via-to-plane
     errors, but every gap is exactly 0.1505 mm. Nothing overlaps, so plotting
     from this fill would not short anything. The previous session's transcript
     called this a short-causing blocker; that was wrong. Still, refill before
     plotting.
  3. No fiducials and no test points. This matters only for machine assembly,
     and you have a 0.5 mm-pitch TQFP-144.
  4. No MPNs on any of the 56 unique parts. This blocks sourcing and any
     datasheet verification.
  5. Few vias under the exposed pads: U101 and U201 have 1 each, U404 has 3,
     U301 has 21. At these power levels it's probably fine, but that's a guess.

  False positives

  - PP-001 (iCE40 VCCPLL pins have no supply path): they're fed from +1V2
    through 100 Ω into 10 µF + 100 nF. That's the usual iCE40 PLL filter (from
    memory); the analyzer's search doesn't go through resistors.
  - VM-001 (voltage-domain crossings): VCMA/VCMB are an analog common-mode
    reference, not logic. AN_EN is a 2N7002 drain pulled up to +3V3.
  - PU-001 (PG pins lack pull-ups): both PG pins are marked no-connect.
  - PS-002 (GND split into 7 islands): the "islands" are U301's ground pads.
    KiCad reports 0 unconnected items.
  - EMC GP-001 (134 nets over a plane gap): I tested this against the raw In1
    fill polygon. /AFE_A/FDA_INN, reported at 0% coverage, is 100% over GND. I
    suspect a KiCad 10 parsing problem in the analyzer but haven't confirmed the
    cause. Either way, this rule's output can't be trusted here; the upstream
    kicad-happy project may want to know.
  - J101/J201 overhanging the edge: horizontal BNCs normally do. Please confirm
    that's intended.
  - ERC/DRC noise:
    - 155 library-symbol and 170 library-footprint mismatches (the libraries
      have changed since the parts were placed).
    - 170 Datasheet-field mismatches (~ vs empty).
    - H1–H4 are not in the schematic.
    - U603's WP and HOLD pins are tied high, which ERC flags.

  Minor, worth a look

  - There's no capacitor on VBUS before the ferrite bead; the 100 nF is on the
    +5V side.
  - The THS4521 inputs run over solid GND. TI's layout advice for this kind of
    amplifier is, as I recall, to clear the plane under the input pins to cut
    stray capacitance. That's from memory and low priority at the LTC2290's 10
    MS/s.
  - The EMC check flags the SDRAM and SPI clocks for changing layers without a
    nearby stitching via (RP-001). That's worth a look on SD_CLK; everything
    else there is probably minor.
