● I've finished the design, but nothing has been built or tested on hardware
  yet. Several parts still rest on unverified assumptions, listed below. The

  What it is:
  - Inputs: two BNC inputs with 1 MΩ input impedance so scope probes work. Each
    channel has an attenuator, a buffer and a 3rd-order anti-alias filter (about
    8.8 MHz, −3 dB).
  - ADC: one ADS5231 dual 12-bit ADC. It won't run below 20 MSPS, so it samples
    at 40 MSPS and the FPGA reduces that to 10 MSPS.
  - FPGA and memory: a Gowin GW1NR-9 FPGA. Its built-in PSRAM holds the 0.1 s
    capture, which is 32 Mbit at 16 bits per sample.
  - USB: an FT232H high-speed USB 2.0 bridge on a USB-C receptacle, wired for
    USB 2.0 only. Streaming both channels at full rate would need 40 MB/s, which
    is at or above what USB 2.0 can sustain, so the board captures to the
    buffer first and then sends it to the host.
  - Power: estimated at 1.47 W. The worst case, with a 1.25× margin, is 496 mA,
    just under the 500 mA USB 2.0 limit. A switch to USB-C wasn't strictly
    needed, but the USB-C connector is there anyway.

  Decisions I made without asking, as the prompt directed (options and reasoning
  are in the handoffs):
  - FPGA I/O bank 3 needs a 1.8 V supply, so I added a +1V8 regulator. The JTAG
    header now references 1.8 V, so your programmer must support 1.8 V.
  - The FPGA had too few 3.3 V-capable pins, so I dropped the ADC's data-valid
    and overrange outputs.
  - The 40 MHz oscillator didn't publish its jitter, so I swapped it for one
    rated at 0.7 ps.
  - The FT232H runs in its 5 V supply mode. In 3.3 V mode the worst-case rail
    (3.21 V) fell below the chip's 3.30 V minimum. The ERC reviewer didn't
    re-check this change; I checked the new net in the netlist myself.

  Still open:
  - BNC footprint (J2/J3): no stock footprint matched, so I drew a custom one
    from a datasheet drawing that isn't to scale. Check it against a physical
    connector before fab.
  - Unverified FPGA facts:
    - The exposed pad goes to GND.
    - Core current is at most 150 mA and the 1.8 V load at most 100 mA. The
      power budget only holds if both are true.
    - The PSRAM can sustain the 40 MB/s needed. The raw rate is 664 MB/s, so
      this is likely, but the throughput of Gowin's PSRAM controller IP isn't
      confirmed.
  - Missing filtering: Gowin recommends a ferrite bead and 4.7 µF on the FPGA

  - Supply risk: the ADC and the FPGA each come from a single supplier with low
