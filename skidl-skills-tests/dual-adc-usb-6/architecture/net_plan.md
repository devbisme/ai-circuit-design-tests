### Rail-isolation ferrites FB1–FB4 (rev.2 — previously unnamed in this file)

All four are BLM21PG601SN1D, 600 Ω @ 100 MHz, 1.4 A, **R_DC = 140 mΩ** (LCSC C41556732,
live-verified). The allocation the `power` block coder assumed is **correct as built**:

| ref | from net | to net | purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FB1` | `VBUS_SW` | `LDO_ADC_IN` | **risk R5's prescription** — ferrite + C11 (10 µF) in front of U5 |
| `FB2` | `VBUS_SW` | `LDO_AMP_IN` | same, in front of U6 |
| `FB3` | `LDO_ADC_OUT` | `+3V3A_ADC` | output-side isolation; C12 (1 µF) before it, C16/C26 after |
| `FB4` | `LDO_AMP_OUT` | `+3V3A_AMP` | same for the amp rail; C15 before, C17/C27 after |

`LDO_ADC_IN`, `LDO_AMP_IN`, `LDO_ADC_OUT`, `LDO_AMP_OUT` are **`power`-internal nets** —
they are not block interface nets and no other block may reference them.

Arithmetic, so nobody re-derives it:

- **Load-regulation cost of FB3:** 55 mA × 0.140 Ω → **7.7 mV** on a 3.300 V rail
  (0.23 %). FB4: 43 mA × 0.140 Ω → **6.0 mV**. Both are inside the ADC's and the amps'
  supply tolerance and are calibrated out by the ADC's own internal reference.
- **Why the output ferrites are kept:** the TPS73633's PSRR collapses above ~100 kHz, and
  the bucks switch at ~1 MHz. At 1 MHz the bead is ~40 Ω against C16‖C26 = 1.1 µF
  (|Z| = 1/(2π·1 MHz·1.1 µF) = 0.145 Ω) → **≈ 49 dB** of extra rejection exactly where the
  LDO has none. That is the R5 mechanism; do not delete FB3/FB4 either.
- **The residual they create** is recorded as risk R5's addendum in `design_risks.md`.

**Stability note (binding on the coder):** the TPS73633's own output capacitor must stay
**between the OUT pin and the ferrite** — C12 for U5, C15 for U6. It is the loop's
compensation cap; putting it after the bead would put a ferrite inside the control loop.
The current code already does this correctly.

### Input protection — where the clamp goes and why (rev.2, replaces the rev.1 arrangement)

<!-- revised: rev.1 put D101/D201 across the BNC centre contact, upstream of both
     current-limiting elements. That broke F2, I3 and I2 simultaneously. -->

**The clamp chain, in order, per channel:**

```
J2.1 ──┬── R100 909k ──┬── R102 1k ──┬── U100.IN+        (CH1_BNC → CH1_ATT → CH1_BUFIN)
       └── C100 22p ───┤             ├── D100  BAV199 pin3, pins 1/2 → +3V3A_AMP / GND
                       │             └── D101  ESD9L5.0ST5G  K → CH1_BUFIN, A → GND
                 R101 90.9k → VREF_OFF
                 C101 220p  → VREF_OFF
```

Nothing is connected to `CHn_BNC` except J, R100 and C100. **The BNC node carries no
semiconductor.** The attenuator itself is the series limiter, and both clamps are behind it.

**What CHn_BUFIN actually does, computed from the real values** (R100 = 909 kΩ,
R101 = 90.9 kΩ, VREF_OFF = 1.807 V; bottom leg returns to VREF_OFF, not GND):

- Divider ratio = 90.9 / (909 + 90.9) = **0.090909 = 1/11.0** (top = R100, bottom = R101).
- `V(BUFIN) = 1.807 + (Vin − 1.807) × 0.090909`
- Vin = **+10 V** → 1.807 + 8.193 × 0.090909 = **2.552 V**
- Vin = **−10 V** → 1.807 − 11.807 × 0.090909 = **0.734 V**

So at the board's own ±10 V full scale (F2, HARD) the clamp node spans 0.734–2.552 V,
entirely below the 5 V standoff of either clamp. **Nothing conducts at full scale.** That
is what rev.1 got wrong: a 5 V / 7.8 V part sitting directly on the ±10 V BNC pin was
2.2 V past breakdown at the rated input.

**±50 V DC continuous (I3, HARD)** — solve the mid node with D100 clamping BUFIN:

- Vin = +50 V: BUFIN pinned at +3.300 + 0.7 = **4.0 V** by D100's upper diode.
  Node currents: (50 − 4.03)/909 kΩ = +50.6 µA in, (4.03 − 1.807)/90.9 kΩ = −24.5 µA out
  through R101 → **≈ 26 µA** into the clamp. D100 is rated 200 mA continuous → **7600×**.
- Vin = −50 V: BUFIN pinned at **−0.7 V**; by the same balance, **≈ 27 µA** into the clamp.
- Worst-case dissipation in R100: I²R = (50.6 µA)² × 909 kΩ = **2.3 µW**; 46 V across an
  0805 rated 150 V working. C100/C101 are 100 V C0G (already sourced that way, rev.5).
- The TVS sits at 4.0 V < its 5 V standoff and **does not conduct during the DC fault at
  all** — a DC overvoltage is the attenuator's and the BAV199's job; the TVS is only there
  for the nanosecond-scale ESD strike that the BAV199 is too slow to catch.

**±20 pF input capacitance (I2, HARD)** — `CHn_BNC` now carries only C100 in series with
C101: 22 pF × 220 pF / 242 pF = **20.0 pF**, and both legs τ-match at
909 kΩ × 22 pF = 90.9 kΩ × 220 pF = **20.0 µs**. Budget is met exactly and the clamp no
longer spends any of it. (Had the clamp been moved to `CHn_ATT` instead, the bottom leg
would have gone to 90.9 kΩ × 235 pF = 21.4 µs and compensation would be broken — so the
clamp goes **behind R102**, never on the mid node.)

**The pole R102 adds at BUFIN, and why the part changed** — see `ic_selection.md`
§"Input ESD clamp (rev.2)" for the full working. Short form: the clamp node's total
capacitance loads R102 = 1.00 kΩ.

| clamp part | C_TVS | node total (TVS + BAV199 ~3 pF + TPH2501 ~3 pF + trace ~1.5 pF) | pole | f_−3dB of the chain | 10 MHz |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ESD9B5.0ST5G (rev.1) | 15 pF | 22.5 pF | 7.07 MHz | **4.08 MHz** nominal, **3.83 MHz** worst-case → **fails F8** | −33 dB |
| **ESD9L5.0ST5G (rev.2)** | **0.9 pF** | **8.4 pF** | **18.9 MHz** | **4.39 MHz** (F8 needs ≥ 4 MHz) | **−30.5 dB** (F9 needs ≥ 25 dB) |
