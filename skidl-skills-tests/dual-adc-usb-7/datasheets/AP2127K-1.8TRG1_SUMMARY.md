## Notes

- **This part's 50 µs typ soft-start cannot be relied on to meet a ≥180 µs VCCIO ramp
  requirement** (see `architecture/power_budget.md`, Gowin DS117 Table 3-3). No max spec is
  given for t_ss, and nothing in the datasheet suggests output capacitance or an EN-pin RC
  slows the internal ramp once Shutdown crosses its 1.5V threshold — see
  `handoffs/04_datasheets.md` rev.6 Decision 16 for the full resolution and the recommended
  downstream fix (second TPS22918 load switch with a CT timing cap, not an RC on Shutdown).
- Application circuit: CIN = 1µF (X7R/X5R ceramic, close to VIN/GND), COUT = 1µF minimum
  (higher is fine for transient response, does not slow ramp).
- Auto-discharge: shutdown version integrates a 60Ω (typ) MOSFET VOUT→GND when disabled.
