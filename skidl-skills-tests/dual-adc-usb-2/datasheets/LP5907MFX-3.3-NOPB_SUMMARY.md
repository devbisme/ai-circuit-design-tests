## Notes

- U4 in `power_analog`: feeds AVDD (AD9235) and the 10 MHz XO (X1) — this is a
  noise-critical rail (`[CRIT][FIXED]` per sourcing), directly affects ADC SNR/ENOB and
  clock jitter.
- Decoupling per sourced BOM: shares C14–C17 (1 µF X7R) group with U5 (REF3025), plus
  per-pin 100 nF (C18–C21 group, shared across U3/U4/U5).
- **EN pin must be tied**, not floating — tie to IN (pin 1) unless a sequencing
  requirement is introduced at coding time.
