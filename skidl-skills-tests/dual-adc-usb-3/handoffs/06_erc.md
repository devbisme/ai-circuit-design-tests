# Phase 6 handoff — ERC & footprint review

**0 errors, 0 warnings, 1 note. Circuit is PASS.** Export is unblocked.

Revision 1 returned FAIL on two HIGH findings. Both are fixed in `analog_frontend.py` and
`clock_gen.py`, both re-verified here from the netlist and from independent analysis, and all
four MEDIUM findings are fixed as well.

## Decisions

1. **H1 accepted as fixed, on my own numbers.** I rebuilt the filter model from scratch — a
   6-node differential MNA solve with an ideal FDA, component values read out of
   `outputs/dual_adc_usb.net` rather than from the coder's handoff — and cascaded it with the
   Sallen-Key. Result: **−2.65 dB @ 4 MHz, −31.43 dB @ 10 MHz, −55.44 dB @ 20 MHz**, DC gain
   1.10, cascade f−3dB 4.08 MHz, passband peaking +0.19 dB. SPEC F6 and F7 both met. This
   reproduces the coder's claim to within 0.05 dB at every frequency. The topology is now the
   classic MFB: C1 (C141/C241, 30 pF) differential across the two node-A's, C2 (C139/C140,
   22 pF) from each amplifier input to its feedback-sense pin.
2. **H2 accepted as fixed, both level directions checked at source.** `CLK_ADC1/2` now swing
   to 3.00 V against the AD9235's `CLK ≤ AVDD+0.3` = 3.30 V (300 mV margin; rev 1 was
   −18 mV). The 3.3 V XO into a 3.0 V-powered SN74LVC2G34 is explicitly supported — the
   datasheet advertises 5.5 V-tolerant inputs and down-translation — and 3.00 V clears the
   AD9235's VIH of 2.0 V. `CLK_FPGA` is tapped ahead of the buffer, so the FPGA's Bank-2 pin
   still sees a full 3.3 V swing. U11's outputs drive nothing else.
3. **M3 pin 75 confirmed Bank 1** against `datasheets/UG803_GW1NR9_Pinout.pdf`:
   `IOT38A  I/O  1  …  75  75`, powered from VCCIO1 (pin 58) = `+3V3`. Pin 11 is back in the
   NC set. **No 3.3 V node touches Bank 3 anywhere in the netlist now** — that was the last
   one.
4. **M2 verified by measurement, not by claim:** two consecutive runs emit an identical
   netlist (md5 `d32d36f10ff8806884ca025406579136`). Deriving each tag from the refdes is the
   right identity for the PCB flow.
5. **Dispositioned as acceptable:** the one remaining generation warning is
   `Missing tag on  instantiated at <frozen importlib._bootstrap>:491` — the top-level circuit
   object, not a component. It does not affect the netlist.
6. **Standing caveat, unchanged and accepted:** `.drive = POWER` is asserted on every
   top-level rail plus `VIN_A`, `+3V0A` and `CLKBUF_VDD`. Every one traces to a real
   regulator output, so nothing is hidden today — but ERC has no coverage for an unpowered
   rail in this design. Any future rail edit must be checked by hand.
7. **H1's margin is thin and that is a design property, not a defect.** 1.4 dB at 10 MHz,
   and ±5 % on C139/C141 is worth roughly ±0.4 dB there. It clears; `design_risks.md` R4
   already flagged this stopband. Prototype measurement should confirm it.

## Carried forward

Nothing blocking, but a human should eyeball these before fabrication:

- **J102/J202 BNC footprint is wrong** and must be rebuilt against a physical part before
  Gerbers. Netlist-complete, layout-blocking. (Was J2 ×2 in phase 5.)
- **SiT1602BI (Y1) pinout unverified** — no datasheet exists; corroborated only against the
  SiT8008 family symbol. Check the pin-1 dot on a physical part.
- **MODE0/MODE1 straps unconfirmed** against DS117E. Strapped low through R49/R50, defined
  and re-strappable.
- **GW1NR-9 EP is 6.8 mm, the footprint's is 6.74 mm**; the AD9235 uses the correct 3.1 mm EP
  land rather than the BOM's 3.45 mm. Layout should check both pads.
- **Only one custom-generated footprint is in use:** `Inductor_SMD_Custom:L_FNR3015S_3.0x3.0mm`
  (L1, L2). Everything else resolves to a stock KiCad library footprint.
- **L1** VCM divider 10k/10k (~5 kΩ source) drives two VOCM pins; actual CM may sit tens of
  mV off 1.500 V. *Uncertain* — VOCM input resistance not confirmed from the datasheet.
- **L2** FB1 (600 R, 500 mA rated) carries the entire board's VBUS current — check rating and
  DCR against the real budget.
- **L3** Firmware must assert PWR_EN before enabling the FX2's slave-FIFO output drivers,
  or U13 drives an unpowered U12.
- **L5 (new)** `sourcing/sourced_bom.md` still names U7/U8/J2/R13-R27/C34-C47 for the front
  end; the netlist now uses the 100/200-series refdes. Quantities are unaffected (still ×2
  each) — documentation sync for purchasing.
- **L6 (new, layout)** U11 now switches on the analog rail. FB6 + 100 nF + 10 µF isolate its
  supply, but the return current shares the single GND plane — keep the clock return away
  from the ADC reference pins.
- **L4** cosmetic: bead footprint strings differ (`Resistor_SMD:R_0603` on FB1/FB2/FB3/FB6,
  `Inductor_SMD:L_0603` on FB4/FB5). Same land pattern.
- **H1 stopband margin is 1.4 dB at 10 MHz.** Confirm on the prototype.
