**Jitter budget holds with wide margin.** The architecture assumed ~2.5 ps rms total
against a 20 ps bar (12 bits at 4 MHz). The XO's own phase jitter is 0.9 ps max. It is
characterised at 75 MHz rather than 10 MHz, but a margin over 20× makes the conclusion
insensitive to that.

## Land pattern (p.10) vs. KiCad footprint — verified

Datasheet recommended land pattern for 3.2 × 2.5 mm: pad pitch **2.2 mm × 1.9 mm**, pad
**1.4 × 1.2 mm**. KiCad `Oscillator_SMD_SiT_PQFN-4Pin_3.2x2.5mm` places pads at
(±1.1, ±0.95) with size 1.4 × 1.2 — **an exact match**. Pad 1 at (−1.1, +0.95).

## Notes

- **Pin 1 handling.** Datasheet note 1 (p.2): in OE mode "a pull-up resistor of 10 kΩ or
  less is recommended if pin 1 is not externally driven." `clock_gen` ties pin 1 directly
  to `+3V3`, which satisfies it (0 Ω). Left floating it would still sit high on the
  internal pull-up, but more weakly than recommended — the direct tie is the right call.
- **Decoupling.** Datasheet note 2 (p.2): "A capacitor of value 0.1 µF or higher between
  Vdd and GND is required." `clock_gen` has C71 = 100 nF on Y1 ✓. Place it at pins 4/2.
- **Load.** OUT drives two LVCMOS buffer inputs plus the 33 Ω `CLK_FPGA` branch. That is
  three CMOS inputs plus traces, roughly 15–20 pF (an estimate, not a measurement). The
  datasheet specifies all limits "with 15 pF output load" (p.2), and the part supports
  60 pF or more through drive-strength selection (p.7). So the load sits near the
  characterisation point: expect edges a little slower than the 15 pF tables at this
  load, which is irrelevant at 10 MHz because the buffer re-squares them.
