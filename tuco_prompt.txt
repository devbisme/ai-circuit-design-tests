Look at all the runs of the KiCad-related AI tools in the subdirectories.
Your goal is to determine the components of these tools that are most effective
for phases 1-14 and combine them into a tool that generates and analyzes KiCad electronic designs
better than these tools. Better can be defined as some combination of:

* Easy to install (i.e. it uses MCPs, skills, scripts and can communicate via KiCad IPC but does not depend upon the KiCad GUI).
* Uses less tokens.
* Finishes more quickly.
* Produces a design that is less expensive to produce.
* Draws from a wider selection of components.
* Considers a wider range of possible design architectures.
* Places components into a smaller area while maintaining routability and signal integrity.
* Produces fully routed PCBs.
* Considers issues related to manufacturability.
