● Yes, it's done now: a complete KiCad 10 project with a fully routed 4‑layer
  board. The weakest point is that signal traces cut through the inner GND
  plane, so the layout isn't finished to the standard a 12‑bit ADC needs.

  Checks:
  - Schematic: KiCad ERC shows 0 errors. Its netlist matches the SKiDL source
    exactly.
  - PCB: KiCad DRC, run with the schematic-consistency check, shows 0 errors, 0
    unconnected pads and 0 mismatches against the schematic. The 30 warnings are
    silkscreen overlaps and 3 footprints that differ slightly from their
    library copies.

  Design choices (all options and reasoning are in executive_summary.md):
  - Inputs: BNC connectors at 1 MΩ‖20 pF, so standard scope probes and leads
    work and 10× probes can be compensated.
  - Input stage: an attenuator biased from a voltage reference, a buffer amp,
    and a differential amplifier driving the ADC. Full scale is ±10.09 V.
  - ADC: LTC2290, dual 12‑bit at 10 MS/s. It runs at its maximum rated speed;
    the 25 MS/s LTC2291 is a drop-in replacement if you want margin.
  - Capture: an iCE40HX4K FPGA writes into 8 MB of SDRAM. That holds 0.2 s at
    full rate; 0.1 s was required.
  - USB: an FT232H gives USB 2.0 high speed through a USB‑B connector. I
    estimate about 260 mA from 5 V, which USB 2.0 can supply, so USB‑C wasn't
    needed. That figure is from datasheet values, not measured.

  What went wrong along the way:
  - Schematic short: the schematic generator drew two parts on top of each other
    and shorted +1V2 to GND. The netlist comparison caught it; it's fixed.
  - Routing: it took four Freerouting runs. The delivered board is from run 3,
    the only one that left nothing unrouted.
  - Rules file: saving the board from pcbnew overwrites the project's design
    rules with defaults, so write_project.py has to be re-run after every save.

  Risks to look at before fabrication:
  - GND plane: Freerouting ran 81 signal nets (about 1.7 m of track) through
    In1, the inner GND plane, including under the ADC. A run that kept In1 clear
    left 4 signals and about 19 GND pads unconnected. The ADC area needs hand
    routing, or a 6‑layer stackup.
  - Not built yet:
    - FPGA logic and host software.
    - The one-time FT232H EEPROM setup that selects FIFO mode.
    - Gerber and drill files.
  - Datasheet values from memory: a few part hookup details, listed in the
    summary, need checking against the datasheets.
  - USB layout and current:
    - The USB data lines aren't routed as a 90 Ω differential pair.
    - The board draws more than the 100 mA USB allows before the host configures
      it.
  - Analog front end: the attenuator capacitors will probably need tuning on the
    first board, and anti-alias filtering is only 2nd order (about 4.4 MHz).
