● I did everything in analysis_prompt.txt, and the review found real problems
  even though ERC and DRC report no errors (only warnings). I didn't change any
  design files. Statements about how parts behave come from what I remember of
  the datasheets, not from documents I checked during this review; the summary
  marks them (datasheet recall).

  Top findings:
  1. Input clamp diodes do nothing (D101/D201). The divider node goes to the
     midpoint of one diode pair whose ends aren't connected, while the other
     pair's ends go to the rails with its midpoint floating. That's certain from
     the netlist as drawn; it's still worth checking the symbol's pin numbering
     against the real part.
  2. Decoupling capacitors are far from their pins. Distances are 5.6–11 mm on
     the ADC, up to 22 mm on the FPGA, 8–14 mm on the SDRAM and 14–16 mm on the
     FT2232H core pins. On the LM27762 (the ±4 V supply) the 8–10 mm distance
     may also hurt its stability, not just add noise.
  3. The FPGA has no clock until USB enumerates. The 10 MHz oscillator is
     powered from the same 3.0 V regulator that the FT2232H's PWREN# signal
     switches on.
  4. Medium-level issues:
     - The FT2232H mode plan may conflict: channel A is wired for sync FIFO and
       channel B for flash programming, and I believe channel B is unavailable
       in sync-FIFO mode.
     - The USB pair isn't routed as a controlled-impedance differential pair.
     - The unpowered ADC can be back-powered through the FPGA's pins.
     - The ±4 V charge pump sits between the two analog channels.

  Fab outputs:
  - No LCSC part numbers: the design has none, so every BOM line must be matched
    in JLC's tool. I haven't checked stock for the specialty chips.
  - Through-hole parts: the connectors are in the placement file, so either
    order through-hole assembly or remove them.
  - Rotations: check every part in JLC's placement preview before ordering,
    because KiCad and JLC define zero rotation differently for many packages.
