● I've finished the review using eda-agent, generated the JLCPCB Gerbers, BOM,
  pick-and-place file and PNG, and written executive_summary.md. The design
  files weren't modified (the board's checksum is unchanged).

  Checks: No ERC or DRC errors and no unconnected items. There are 343 DRC
  warnings and 203 ERC warnings, all cosmetic, library-related or caused by the
  environment.

  Most serious problems (19 ranked in the summary):
  1. FPGA clock is switched off until USB enumerates. The 10 MHz oscillator runs
     from the 3.0 V analog rail, which stays off until then. The ADC's output
     supply is on the same rail, so the powered FPGA may back-power the
     unpowered ADC through its data lines.
  2. ADC inputs can be overdriven. The differential driver runs from 0/+4 V into
     a 3.0 V ADC, so roughly ±38 V at a BNC exceeds the ADC's input limit. That
     limit is from memory, not checked against the datasheet.
  3. Decoupling caps are far from the pins. On the FPGA, 14 of 16 supply pins
     are more than 5 mm from a cap (worst 22 mm); on the ADC, 11 of 19 (worst
     10.8 mm). eda-agent's review missed this because it only checks that caps
     exist.
  4. Anti-alias caps are 16–18 mm from the ADC. The ADC input traces run beyond
     them over the 3.3 V digital plane rather than ground.
  5. Two LM27762 datasheet deviations (checked against the datasheet): the 49.9
     kΩ feedback resistors are below the required 50 kΩ minimum, and the unused
     PGOOD pin is left unconnected instead of grounded.
  6. The BOM can't be ordered as is. The schematic has no part-number or LCSC
     fields. Silkscreen strokes are 0.12 mm, below JLC's 0.15 mm minimum as I
     recall it.

  I checked the BNC input clamp diodes because KiCad's pin names make them look
  miswired. The Diodes datasheet diagram shows the wiring is correct.

  Where eda-agent fell short (I filled the gaps):
  - Its full review cuts DRC and ERC lists off at 200 rows, so I reran both
    through its kicad_cli passthrough.
  - Its pad list has no component references, so I measured decoupling distances
    with KiCad's bundled Python instead.
  - Its exports aren't in JLC format. Gerbers include every layer, the BOM has
    one ungrouped row per part, and the position file is in inches. I zipped
    only the fabrication layers and converted the BOM and placement file myself.

  Unverified claims: I couldn't download the FT2232H datasheet (FTDI returned
  403) or the LTC2290 and ADA4817 datasheets (analog.com timed out). Claims that
  rest on them are marked "from memory" in the summary, including the USB
  bandwidth margin and FT2232H channel B being unavailable during streaming.
